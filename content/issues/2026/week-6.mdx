Alex is 6–0. Four teams are 4–2. Everybody else is already explaining something.

That is a two-game lead with eight regular-season weeks still to play. Nobody has clinched anything. Alex has simply made the rest of us look like we are participating in a different subscription tier.

Joel beat Jacob 145.43–119.06 and took the weekly scoring crown. Jacob still leads Joel in season points, 878.63 to 878.43. Two-tenths of a point separate them after six weeks. They are also tied at 53–13 in all-play. The defending champion and the Supreme Commissioner are conducting an argument that requires a calculator.

Kyle has won four straight. Brandon has won one, which is a considerably bigger improvement than that sentence makes it sound. Jordan beat Deco while scoring 90.92. I beat Hutch by 0.13, which technically counts as a winning margin even if it looks more like sales tax.

Then the injury news arrived, and several of next week’s lineups stopped resembling this week’s.

Alex keeps winning. The rest of us would like to speak to customer service.

## From the Chat: The League Discovers Ctrl+Z

We spent the week asking whether grown men should be held responsible for pressing buttons.

I opened the hearing by dropping Jake Ferguson when I meant to add Pat Freiermuth without doing that. An ambitious transaction. Most people acquire one tight end. I managed to create a constitutional question.

Jacob immediately began preparing for the auction. “Good luck out bidding me,” he said, which is exactly the sympathy you want while watching a friend measure your furniture for his living room.

Alex suggested that anyone who reads this newsletter should have seen the mistake coming. Fair. I have accidentally built a weekly publication that serves as evidence against my ability to operate the app.

So I appealed to the Supreme Leader. Ferguson came back through a commissioner adjustment. Freiermuth came off, then returned in a separate move for Dohnte Meyers. Two tight ends, several transactions and one government intervention later, I had accomplished something other owners apparently do in one attempt.

Then Brandon brought a second case before the court.

The pending deal would have sent Cam Skattebo and Brandon’s 2028 third to Deco for Kyle Monangai. It was canceled. Nothing changed hands. Brandon explained that he had told Joel immediately it wasn’t what he meant to send.

Deco responded, “What is this bullshit??”

That is a substantially shorter appeal than mine, but I respect the formatting.

By this point, Joel’s commissioner duties had expanded from managing the league to providing technical support for people who can identify a dynasty asset but cannot reliably click one. Jacob’s Ferguson auction never happened. Deco’s pending trade never became a trade. Sam had already reviewed the Ferguson rescue: “Booooooooooo.”

I cannot argue with the crowd.

I can only suggest that Sleeper move the confirmation button farther away from whatever all of us are actually trying to press.
