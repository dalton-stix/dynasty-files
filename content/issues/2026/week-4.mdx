Jacob finally lost.

Alex finally answered something on time.

After three weeks of Jacob, Deco and Alex occupying the unbeaten table, Week 4 removed the defending champion's chair. Alex beat Jacob 140.91–131.38 and improved to 4–0. Deco scored 159.92, buried Hutch by 43.02 and joined him.

Those are the only two perfect teams left.

They got there differently. Alex has won four games with balanced production and just enough answers at the right time. Deco has won four games while building a bench that appears capable of operating a second franchise without regulatory approval.

Jacob's loss was not a collapse. He scored 131.38, finished sixth in the league for the week and still leads everybody with 622.77 points. Alex simply beat him. That's a sentence our trade inbox may receive a response to sometime in December.

JP led the league with 161.23. Matt scored 159.39, nearly doubled Jordan and reminded everybody that one terrible month is not legally binding. Joel and Kyle also won. Brandon and Sam are now 0–4.

Then Wednesday arrived, and I spent $20 on Joe Mixon several hours after the Seahawks had decided not to sign him following a physical. By Wednesday afternoon, I had cut him too.

The newsletter remains committed to accountability, unfortunately.

- JP paid **$6 for Will Shipley**. Kyle's $0 claim failed. With Saquon week to week and Tank Bigsby headed to NFL IR, this is a real contingency purchase. It is not a guaranteed workload.
- JP paid **$1 for Dallas's defense** and dropped Carolina. Matt's $0 claim failed.
- Matt added Washington's defense for **$0** and dropped Philadelphia.
- Alex paid **$1 for Ka'imi Fairbairn** and dropped Kalif Raymond.
- I paid **$20 for Joe Mixon** and dropped San Francisco's defense.

That final move needs a timeline.

[NFL, October 6](https://amp.nfl.com/news/report-seahawks-opt-against-signing-rb-joe-mixon-following-physical).
