I went 5–1 on last week's picks.

The only team I got wrong was mine.

Jacob won. Joel won. Deco won. Hutch won. Alex won. Then JP beat me 135.72–103.01, completing an otherwise excellent week of football analysis by demonstrating exactly where the conflict of interest lives.

Last week I said one of us was about to develop a strong opinion about sample size.

Two games is a very small sample size.

Meanwhile, Joel followed his official state portrait with 170.90 points and the highest score in the league. Jacob is still undefeated, still leading total scoring, and still in possession of the trophy. Five teams are 2–0. Five are 0–2. Matt and JP are standing in the middle, having arrived from different directions.

The closest matchup was decided by 27.67. Three were decided by more than 70.

None of the final scoreboards required a recount. Several of us needed a different Sunday.

That's a difficult problem to solve in the editor.
