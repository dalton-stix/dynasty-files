## Joelonomics: The 2026 Scoring Revolution

The headline story of this offseason isn't a trade or a rookie draft — it's scoring. Commissioner Joel Wiggins proposed a large slate of scoring changes aimed at balancing positional value and making more player archetypes viable across all twelve rosters. It sparked heavy debate in league chat, the kind that only a group this online for a fake sport could sustain for weeks.

Two proposals hit the table:

Joel's weekly median matchup proposal — which would have added a second, league-wide opponent every week based on the scoring median — went down 7–5. Close, but the league said no.

The broader scoring changes were a different story. Those were overwhelmingly approved, and the league will run them as a one-year trial for the 2026 season. Whether "Joelonomics" ends up remembered as a smart rebalancing or a quantitative-easing-style overcorrection is exactly the kind of thing this newsletter exists to track. Ask again in December.

It was Deco's third straight trip to the title game — a streak that's turned Drunken Monkeys into the roster everyone else in the league is building against, on paper, whether or not the results back that up.
