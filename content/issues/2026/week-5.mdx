Alex is 5–0.

Deco is not.

The undefeated table had two chairs last week. Jacob removed Deco’s with a 136.80–129.57 win, and he did it while Patrick Mahomes contributed zero. Alex handled Jordan 154.39–111.97 and became the only perfect team left.

That should be the whole opening.

Then I scored 202.53.

One week after telling the league chat, “My season is fucked anyways,” my roster produced the first 200-point score of our season and beat Brandon by 121.64. Every non-defense starter except Jake Ferguson scored at least 21.02. Jordan Love scored 30.36 on my bench because even the unnecessary decisions worked.

This does not make me a contender. It makes me 2–3 with spectacular timing.

Matt scored 158.97 and won his second straight. Hutch scored 161.32 and handed Joel his second loss. Kyle won his third in a row. Jacob remains the league’s scoring leader. Sam and Brandon fell to 0–5, and the schedule has now forced them to play each other.

The unbeaten table has one seat. The winless basement is about to lose a tenant.

## From the Chat: Please Stop Encouraging Me

Matt, this is partly your fault.

The league had finally gotten me into a manageable condition. I had wasted $20 on Joe Mixon, declared my season dead and provided enough material to keep everyone comfortable for a while. A little humility. Some personal growth. Possibly even a normal Wednesday.

Then Matt saw my score and asked, “Have we had a 200 burger dropped before?”

You cannot ask a man with his own newsletter whether he just made history. There are no editors here. There is no adult who can take the laptop away.

Within minutes, I was in the chat announcing, “Needed to remind everyone who I am.” Apparently everyone needed reminding that I was the guy who had been wrong about Joe Mixon four days earlier. My season was dead Wednesday. By Sunday it had a publicist.

Joel only made the situation worse. Last week, the Supreme Commissioner was questioning whether I understood how NFL employment worked. By Tuesday, he was reporting that his archive check showed the league had never had a 200-point score. I’m leaving the all-time certification with Joel, but I appreciate his transition from prosecuting attorney to museum curator.

Then Sleeper named me Best Manager. One week after I paid $20 for Joe Mixon’s unemployment. Finally, an awards committee that respects range.

The app was less generous with everyone else’s dignity. Deco got Highest-Scoring Loser, which is a lovely way to tell a man his suffering was statistically impressive. Kyle got Lowest-Scoring Winner. Even his congratulations came with a little asterisk-shaped middle finger. He still gets the win. Deco gets a certificate explaining why he’s pissed.

Meanwhile, Alex is 5–0. Five games, five wins, an actual case for sustained excellence. I have two wins and a commissioner willing to check the archives.

Naturally, I’m the one giving a comeback speech.

Please stop encouraging me. Or at least wait until I’m above .500.
